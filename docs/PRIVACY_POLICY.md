# JOHN MUSIC PLAYER — Privacy Policy
Wanyama John Technologies · Version 1.0.0

**Summary:** the app works entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit or sell any data.

- **No network access.** The app declares no INTERNET permission.
- **No account, ads or analytics.** None are included.
- **Your media stays in place.** Music and videos are read through Android's MediaStore (or folders you choose with the system picker) and are never copied or uploaded.
- **Data stored locally:** playlists, favourites, play history, saved queue, lyrics you load, equalizer and appearance settings. It lives in the app's private storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app. Play history can be cleared in Settings.
- **Permissions:** *Audio/Video (READ_MEDIA_*; READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12L and below)* — list and play your files. *Notifications* — playback controls. *Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)* — required by Android for the audio visualizer to read the audio being played; audio is not recorded, stored or sent. Each permission is requested only when the related feature is used and the app explains why.
- **Folders you select** (file browser, lyrics folder) are accessed read-only through Android's Storage Access Framework; you can remove them at any time.
- **The app never modifies or deletes your media files.**
